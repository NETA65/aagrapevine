Since **January 1, 2027**, the new prices AA Grapevine announced are in effect: a yearly Grapevine subscription costs **$39.00** in print or **$34.00** digital, a yearly La Viña subscription **$19.50** in print or **$17.00** digital, and every Grapevine and La Viña book **$2.00 more**.

All current prices, and how to order from the official stores: [Shop — subscriptions and prices](/shop/#subscriptions). AA Grapevine's notice: [Grapevine & La Viña Pricing Update](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1S2PrbkYC4auxUSlgdwpoQKwZW3QeaI-p/view).

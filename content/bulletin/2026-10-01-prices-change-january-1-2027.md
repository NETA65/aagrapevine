From **January 1, 2027**, a yearly Grapevine subscription costs **$39.00** in print or **$34.00** digital, a yearly La Viña subscription **$19.50** in print or **$17.00** digital, and every Grapevine and La Viña book **$2.00 more**.

AA Grapevine announced the new prices in a letter to Intergroups and Central Offices. It explains that the costs of producing and distributing its publications and products keep rising: printing, paper and materials, fulfillment, postage and shipping.

**What it means for your group.** If your group buys Grapevine or La Viña books or subscriptions, your treasurer may want to plan next year's budget with the new prices. GVRs and RLVs: a short heads-up at your group's business meeting helps everyone plan ahead.

- Today's prices and the new ones, side by side: [Shop — subscriptions and prices](/shop/#subscriptions)
- AA Grapevine's notice: [Grapevine & La Viña Pricing Update](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1S2PrbkYC4auxUSlgdwpoQKwZW3QeaI-p/view)

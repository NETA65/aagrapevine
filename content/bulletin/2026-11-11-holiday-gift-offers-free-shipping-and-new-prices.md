AA Grapevine and La Viña have announced holiday gift offers, free shipping and new prices for 2027. The gift offers and free shipping are for U.S. and Canadian addresses and run through December 31, 2026.

AA Grapevine says neither of its two offers can be combined with any other offer.

GVRs and RLVs: if anyone in your group orders Grapevine or La Viña books, subscriptions or gifts, a short mention at your group's business meeting lets everyone know.

# Holiday gift offers

Through **December 31, 2026**, AA Grapevine and La Viña each have a holiday gift offer: with a 1-year print subscription at its regular price, for yourself or as a gift, a second 1-year print subscription as a gift is **$16.00** at Grapevine and **$8.00** at La Viña.

Both offers are for U.S. and Canadian addresses, and the details are on each store's page. AA Grapevine says its offer can't be combined with any other offer and is available only through its page.

- **Grapevine:** the first is $36.00, the second $16.00. [Grapevine's offer page](https://www.aagrapevine.org/2gifts) · [Grapevine's page for Canada](https://www.aagrapevine.org/canada-2gifts)
- **La Viña:** the first is $18.00, the second $8.00. [La Viña's offer page](https://www.aalavina.org/2regalos) (in Spanish) · [La Viña's page for Canada](https://www.aalavina.org/canada-2regalos) (in Spanish)
- Both on our Shop page: [Shop — holiday gift offers](/shop/#offers-holiday-gifts-2026)

# Free shipping

From **November 1 through December 31, 2026**, AA Grapevine and La Viña offer **free shipping on all products** to U.S. and Canadian addresses. AA Grapevine says its offer can't be combined with any other offer.

- Both stores and what each one says: [Shop — free shipping](/shop/#offers-free-shipping-2026)
- [Grapevine store](https://www.aagrapevine.org/store)
- [La Viña store](https://www.aalavina.org/tienda) (in Spanish)

# New prices from January 1, 2027

From **January 1, 2027**, a yearly Grapevine subscription costs **$39.00** in print or **$34.00** digital, a yearly La Viña subscription **$19.50** in print or **$17.00** digital, and every Grapevine and La Viña book **$2.00 more**.

AA Grapevine announced the new prices in a letter to Intergroups and Central Offices. It explains that the costs of producing and distributing its publications and products keep rising: printing, paper and materials, fulfillment, postage and shipping.

**What it means for your group.** If your group buys Grapevine or La Viña books or subscriptions, your treasurer may want to plan next year's budget with the new prices.

A Spanish-language workshop on writing your story for La Viña, hosted by Grupo Nuevo Horizonte in Fort Worth and organized by the District 90 La Viña committee: guidelines for contributing to La Viña, how to write your story, new products, trivia, and questions and answers, with members sharing what it was like to see their story published.

Area 65 assembly (Panel 77) at the DoubleTree by Hilton Fort Worth South. The assembly is all day Saturday and Sunday morning (8 AM to 12 PM). There is no activity at the hotel on Friday night, though the host districts may hold something at a nearby group (to be confirmed). District 64 has confirmed as co-host; District 62 has put off its discussion until its November district meeting.
